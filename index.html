Explainable AI for Recruitment

Making AI-powered recruitment more ethical, transparent and human.

EthicaHR is developing an AI-based approach to candidate evaluation designed around explainability, transparency and fairness.

Discover EthicaHR
Our Vision

A recruitment process where every AI decision can be understood.

To make AI in recruitment transparent, explainable and accountable, so technology can support fairer hiring decisions.
The Problem

Recruitment AI is powerful.
But can its decisions be explained?

AI is increasingly being used to support recruitment and candidate evaluation. Yet many systems operate as black boxes, making it difficult to understand why a candidate was selected or rejected.

01

Black-box decisions

Traditional AI-based screening systems can produce decisions without providing a clear explanation of which factors contributed to the outcome.

02

Lack of transparency

Recruiters and organizations may struggle to inspect and communicate how automated evaluation systems reach their conclusions.

03

Difficult-to-audit processes

When AI is involved in recruitment, organizations need processes that can be examined, documented and understood.

04

Human potential gets overlooked

An opaque screening process can make it harder to understand the individual factors behind an evaluation and potentially overlook valuable candidates.

Who We Serve

Built for organizations that need explainable recruitment AI.

Our initial target is SMEs and boutique recruitment agencies, particularly organizations with fewer than 50 employees that may not have dedicated legal or AI engineering teams but still need to understand and document their recruitment processes.

Our Product

From AI decisions to understandable evidence.

EthicaHR aims to develop an explainable AI solution for recruitment that translates complex model decisions into structured and understandable reports.

Explainable AI

We use Explainable AI techniques to identify the contribution of individual variables to a model's decision.

Transparent Evaluation

Instead of presenting only an outcome, the system is designed to provide information that helps users understand the reasoning behind the evaluation.

Bias-Aware Design

The MVP focuses on making the evaluation process more inspectable and supporting the analysis of potential discriminatory effects.

MVP 0.1

How the first version works.

The first MVP is designed to demonstrate how explainability can be integrated into AI-assisted candidate evaluation and converted into a usable XAI report.

01

Candidate Data

Relevant candidate information is provided to the evaluation system.

02

AI Evaluation

The model processes the available variables and produces an evaluation.

03

SHAP Analysis

SHAP-based analysis identifies the contribution of individual variables to the model output.

04

XAI Report

The resulting information is transformed into a structured report designed to make the evaluation easier to understand and inspect.

Explainability is not an extra layer.

It is at the core of the MVP. EthicaHR aims to translate complex decision vectors into understandable mathematical and technical evidence that can support the evaluation process.

AI & Regulation

Recruitment AI needs transparency and accountability.

The regulatory environment surrounding artificial intelligence is making transparency, documentation and risk management increasingly important for organizations using AI in employment-related contexts.

EthicaHR is designed to address this challenge by making AI-assisted evaluation more explainable and inspectable.

Explainability

Understand the factors contributing to an AI evaluation rather than relying solely on a black-box output.

Documentation

Generate structured technical information that can support the documentation and analysis of an AI-assisted process.

Verifiability

Make the underlying evaluation process easier to inspect and scientifically analyse.

Responsible deployment

Consider regulatory and ethical requirements as part of product development rather than as an afterthought.

EthicaHR is developing a technical solution for explainability and verification. It does not represent itself as an official AI Act certification body.
